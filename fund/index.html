SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1

How the fund works. An ADGM private equity fund for Scottish real assets.

Investors commit to a single fund domiciled in Abu Dhabi Global Market. The fund owns each asset through its own Scottish company and returns cash to investors as value is realised.

Lifecycle

The life of the fund. Six stages, each with its own discipline.

01Commitment

Investors commit capital to Fund 1 under the offering documents after eligibility, KYC and diligence.

02Drawdown

Capital is called as investments are approved, rather than held idle.

03Acquisition

Assets are bought below estimated strategic value, after independent valuation and legal, financial and technical diligence.

04Value creation

Operations, contracts, brands and capital structure are improved against a defined plan with measurable milestones.

05Realisation

Value is realised through refinancing, strategic sale or longer-term ownership, assessed against investor objectives. No exit route is assumed.

06Distribution

Cash returns to investors and the general partner as set out in the fund’s distribution waterfall.

The LP / GP programme

Aligned capital. Shared risk, shared reward.

Limited partnersWhat investors receive
  • Control exposure to scarce Scotch whisky and hospitality assets, each held in its own ring-fenced company
  • A preferred return to investors before any carried interest, on a whole-fund basis
  • An LP advisory committee with consent rights over conflicts and related-party transactions
  • Quarterly reporting in ILPA format, annual audited accounts and independent valuation of stock, property and plant
  • Co-investment opportunities alongside Fund 1, offered on a deal-by-deal basis
General partner & sponsorWhat the sponsor commits
  • Sponsor capital committed alongside investors
  • Carried interest earned only after investors’ capital and preferred return are returned
  • Key-person provisions linked to the sponsor’s time commitment
  • Recusal, independent valuation and arm’s-length terms on any transaction with sponsor-affiliated businesses
  • Origination, acquisition and value-creation leadership, with capital released against defined milestones
