Investment strategy

Tangible assets. Transformative ownership.

Control investments in Scotch whisky and hospitality: acquire below strategic value, restore operating performance, build brands and destinations that last.

The thesis

Value in the asset. Potential in the business.

Scotch whisky infrastructure and maturing stock cannot be created quickly. When ownership is under pressure, they can be acquired below their strategic value. Private equity ownership adds the capital, governance and operating focus to close that gap.

We seek opportunities where financial distress, underinvestment or operational underperformance can be addressed through a disciplined acquisition price, appropriate capital and a clear turnaround plan.

Whisky assets, hotel acquisitions and greenfield projects are underwritten separately. Their inclusion in a shared strategy does not make their risks, timelines or sources of return interchangeable.

The method

Six disciplines. One ownership approach.

01Acquire with discipline

Assess land, property, distilling infrastructure, maturing inventory and hotel assets against independent valuation, condition, ownership and the capital needed to restore performance.

02Structure capital around the asset

Match financing and liquidity to the business. Whisky maturation, hotel recovery and greenfield construction have different cash-flow profiles and funding needs.

03Turn operations around

Improve distillery utilisation and commercial contracts; strengthen hotel management, service, procurement, room yield and operating margins.

04Build brands and demand

Develop whisky brands and distribution alongside hotel positioning, guest acquisition and distinctive food, beverage and wellness experiences.

05Develop with defined milestones

For the Rosslyn Distillery greenfield project, phase capital against planning, cost validation, operator selection, construction and independently assessed sustainability objectives.

06Steward and realise value

Use consistent governance and reporting while assessing each asset on its own economics. Consider refinancing, strategic sale or longer-term ownership as performance and investor objectives allow.

Sources of value

Assets, earnings and future potential.

Tangible foundationsLand, infrastructure & inventory

Freehold land, buildings, plant and maturing whisky provide an asset base to assess independently. Whisky may gain value as it matures, subject to quality, demand, storage costs and evaporation.

Operating revenuesProduction, brands & hospitality

Distilling, bulk supply, contract services and branded sales sit alongside hotel rooms, food and beverage, events and wellness services, where supported by the asset’s commercial plan.

Platform valueConsolidation & development

Bring assets together under common ownership, management and distribution, and develop new destinations through phased capital and distinct delivery milestones.

Discipline throughout the hold

A phased path to value creation.

01

Underwrite

Confirm ownership, physical condition, independent values, operating demand and a fully costed investment plan.

02

Stabilise

Secure appropriate financing, retain critical expertise and establish cash-flow and operating controls.

03

Transform

Execute the asset-specific turnaround or development plan, releasing capital against measurable milestones.

04

Realise

Assess strategic sale, refinancing or longer-term ownership against investor objectives and market conditions. No exit route is assumed.

This describes the fund’s investment mandate. Asset appreciation, annual whisky value increases, operating revenues and exits are not guaranteed. Development introduces additional planning, construction and funding risk.
