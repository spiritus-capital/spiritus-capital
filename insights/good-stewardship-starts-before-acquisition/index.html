Stewardship

Back to all insights

A specialist investment strategy brings concentration as well as expertise. Understanding a sector does not remove the need to test assumptions about price, financing, operations and exit.

The SPIRITUS framework starts with financial and legal diligence, independent asset valuation and a reconciled funding plan. Acquisition, operating and development decisions each need appropriate approval gates.

Where the investment team has interests in connected businesses, those relationships must be explicit. Arm’s-length commercial terms, decision-maker recusal and investor oversight are part of the approach.

Fund governance, service-provider appointments and reporting obligations are set out in the fund documents, and are reported on to investors.
