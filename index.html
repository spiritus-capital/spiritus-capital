01 / The firm

A private equity firm. An owner’s discipline.

SPIRITUS Capital is a private equity platform making control investments in Scotch whisky and hospitality. We buy tangible assets below their strategic value, improve how they operate and build them into businesses that last.

Capital is pooled in Abu Dhabi Global Market. Assets are owned and operated in Scotland. The combination gives international family offices and institutions access to a scarce asset class through a familiar common-law fund structure.
