Information about the purpose of this website, SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1 and how enquiries are handled.

Information only

This website describes the activities of SPIRITUS Capital and SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1, an ADGM Qualified Investor Fund offered by private placement to Professional Clients only. It is not an offer to subscribe, a prospectus or personalised investment advice. Any investment would be governed by definitive offering and constitutional documents, subject to eligibility and jurisdictional restrictions.

Fund status and appointments

SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1 has secured £100m of commitments in its first tranche. The first acquisition is targeted to complete in Q1 2027, and the Rosslyn Distillery greenfield project has entered its design phase. The fund is in discussion with prospective investors for the remaining £100m, with a final close targeted for Q3 2027.

The fund manager of SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1 is Oros Asset Management Limited, regulated by the Financial Services Regulatory Authority in ADGM (FSP 220084). Any investment is governed by the fund’s offering and constitutional documents.

Targets, opportunities and risk

The £200m figure is the fund’s target size; £100m was committed in the first tranche. The remaining commitments are not yet secured. Acquisition opportunities are not completed fund holdings. The mandate includes Scotch whisky assets, hotel turnarounds and greenfield hospitality development. Development concepts are subject to approvals, financing and delivery. Net zero is a design and operating ambition, not a verified certification or achieved outcome. Whisky does not necessarily appreciate each year; stock value depends on quality, market demand, carrying costs and realisation. Capital is at risk, investments may be illiquid and no return or exit is guaranteed.

Domicile and regulation

SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1 is domiciled in Abu Dhabi Global Market. Interests in the fund are offered only by private placement to persons who qualify as Professional Clients, subject to the applicable minimum subscription. Nothing on this website is directed at retail investors or at persons in any jurisdiction where its communication would be unlawful.

SPIRITUS Capital is the trading name of SPIRITUS UK Holdings Limited, registered in Scotland (company number SC901738), through which the fund’s UK investments are held. Rosewood Capital Pte. Ltd. acts under a placement mandate; it is not the fund manager.

ADGM regulatory notice

The Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) of Abu Dhabi Global Market has no responsibility for reviewing or verifying any offering document or other document in connection with SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1. Accordingly, the FSRA has not approved the offering documents or any other associated documents, nor taken any steps to verify the information set out in them, and has no responsibility for them. Interests in the fund may be illiquid and subject to restrictions on their resale. Prospective investors should conduct their own due diligence on the interests. If you do not understand the contents of this website, you should consult an authorised financial adviser.

United States and United Kingdom

Interests in the fund have not been and will not be registered under the US Securities Act of 1933 or the securities laws of any US state, and the fund will not be registered under the US Investment Company Act of 1940. Nothing on this website is an offer of, or a solicitation to buy, interests in the fund to or for the account of any US person.

In the United Kingdom, this website is directed only at persons who have professional experience in matters relating to investments (investment professionals within Article 19 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005) and at high net worth companies and other persons within Article 49 of that Order. Any investment activity to which it relates is available only to such persons. Persons who do not have professional experience in matters relating to investments should not rely on it.

Related businesses

Rutland Square Spirits and Rosslyn Distillery are related operating and development businesses. Their revenue, cash, assets and forecasts must not be treated as Fund 1 ownership or performance. Any participation or commercial arrangement remains subject to the relevant documents and conflicts controls.

Images and editorial content

Sector and project imagery illustrates the strategy and does not imply completed fund ownership. Insight articles explain the investment approach; they are not a verified performance record or independent investment research.

Privacy notice

Data controller. SPIRITUS UK Holdings Limited (trading as SPIRITUS Capital), a company registered in Scotland with number SC901738, registered office Bilston Park Industrial Estate, Dryden Road, Loanhead EH20 9LZ. Contact: ir@spiritus.capital.

What we collect. The name, email address, organisation, country, enquiry type and message you choose to send through the enquiry form or by email, and the time you sent it.

Applicable law. We process personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018 and, where it applies to people in the European Economic Area, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

Why, and on what basis. To respond to your enquiry and, where you are an investor or prospective investor, to manage that relationship. The lawful basis is our legitimate interests in responding to and managing professional enquiries. Where an enquiry leads to investor onboarding, eligibility and KYC checks are carried out separately under the fund’s own documentation.

Who receives it. The SPIRITUS team, and service providers who host this website and process enquiries on our behalf under contract. Where an investor relationship is progressed, relevant details may be shared with the fund manager, Oros Asset Management Limited (ADGM), and with the placement adviser, Rosewood Capital Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). Transfers outside the UK and EEA are made only with appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or to countries with an adequacy decision. You may ask us for a copy of the safeguards used.

How long we keep it. For as long as needed to handle the enquiry and in any case no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless a longer period is required by law or by an investor relationship. Data is then securely deleted or anonymised. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Your rights. You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to or restrict its use, or ask for a copy in a portable format, by emailing ir@spiritus.capital. We respond within one month. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or, if you are in the EEA, to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Security. This website is served only over an encrypted connection, and enquiry data is accessible only to those who need it.

Cookies. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. When you confirm your investor status to view key terms, that choice is held in your browser for the session only. Fonts are served from this website, not from third parties.
