Who we are

Owners, not advisers.Buy, build, hold with purpose.

SPIRITUS Capital sponsors SPIRITUS Capital Fund 1, a £200m ADGM-domiciled private equity fund. The fund acquires control of tangible whisky and hospitality assets and takes responsibility for their performance.

The mandate spans operating distilleries and maturing stock, distressed hotels and heritage estates, and greenfield destination development. Each investment is underwritten on its own economics and held within one active-ownership approach.

Founded by Nishant Sharma, managing partner and sponsor, SPIRITUS combines an operator’s knowledge of Scotch whisky with an institutional framework for investment decisions, independent oversight and investor reporting.

Two centres

Abu Dhabi and Edinburgh.Capital meets the asset base.

Abu Dhabi Global MarketFund domicile & fund manager

Fund 1 is domiciled in ADGM, an international financial centre applying English common law. It is home to the fund, to its FSRA-regulated fund manager, Oros Asset Management, and to the fund’s dialogue with family offices and institutions.

Edinburgh, ScotlandAssets & operations

The fund’s investments are held and operated in Scotland, close to the distilleries, warehouses, estates and people that create their value.

SPIRITUS Capital is the trading name of SPIRITUS UK Holdings Limited, a company registered in Scotland (SC901738), through which Fund 1’s UK investments are held.
