Portfolio & pipeline

Acquire with conviction.Rebuild with purpose.

Fund 1 deploys capital across three pillars: Scotch whisky assets, hospitality turnarounds and the Rosslyn Distillery destination.

Deployment

The first acquisition.Targeted for Q1 2027.

With the first tranche completed, Fund 1 is progressing its first acquisition, targeted to complete in Q1 2027. Further opportunities are under review across each pillar.

Asset identities, valuations and commercial terms are confidential. They are shared only with eligible investors under NDA, through the fund’s data room. Opportunities remain subject to diligence, financing, investment approval and binding agreements, and are not completed fund holdings.

Pipeline by pillar

What we look for.Tangible assets with turnaround potential.

01 / Scotch whisky assetsOperating distilleries & stock

Distressed or undervalued whisky businesses with land, distilling infrastructure, maturing inventory and brand potential.

  • Land, property, plant and production capacity
  • Maturing stock with potential for value growth over time
  • Bulk supply, contract production and associated services
  • Owned brands, bottling and distribution opportunities
02 / Hospitality turnaroundsHotels & heritage estates

Distressed hotels and heritage estates where refurbishment, stronger management and clear positioning can restore performance.

  • Assess property condition and local demand
  • Reposition, refurbish and strengthen service standards
  • Improve room yield, occupancy and operating margins
  • Develop food, beverage, events and wellness revenues

03 / Landmark destination

Rosslyn Distillery.A place to restore.

The Rosslyn Distillery greenfield project, in design phase: a landmark destination in Scotland’s Lowlands bringing whisky, luxury hospitality, a wellness centre and retreat experiences into one considered setting.

The ambition is a destination that connects the capital city’s international appeal with Scottish landscape, craft and restorative experiences.

Whisky & provenance

A Lowland identity

Distilling, interpretation and tasting experiences can connect visitors to the place, its craft and its brands.

Luxury & wellbeing

A destination for restoration

A luxury hospitality and retreat proposition with a wellness centre, supported by a defined operator and guest-experience strategy.

Development discipline

An ambition with clear gates

Planning, environmental assessment, independently validated costs, operator agreements and staged financing must precede delivery.

Net zero is a design and operating ambition. Its scope, baseline and delivery pathway require technical assessment and independent validation. The project is proposed, not an operating destination or completed Fund 1 holding. Rosslyn Distillery is a sponsor-affiliated project; any fund participation is subject to related-party approval. No net-zero certification is claimed.
